# Brand & Visual Identity Guide: DotNet.AutoNormalize

## 1. Brand Concept & Positioning

* **Project**: DotNet.AutoNormalize
* **Tagline**: *Normalize once. Everywhere.*
* **GitHub Organization**: `SkyDevLab`
* **Visual Metaphor**: Canonical alignment. Irregular, raw, unstructured data elements (dots and broken fragments) flowing into a precise transformation funnel, emerging as perfectly aligned, parallel, structured representations.

## 3. Typography

## 5. Usage & Co-Branding Rules

- **Clear Space**: Maintain minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter "D" in DotNet.
- **Minimum Size**:
  - Horizontal logo: Minimum width 180px for digital screens.
  - Icon: Minimum size 16 × 16px (favicon) to 512 × 512px (NuGet package icon).
- **Prohibited Alterations**:
  - Do not compress or stretch the aspect ratio.
  - Do not invert or scramble the canonicalization bar direction (transformation must read from left to right).
